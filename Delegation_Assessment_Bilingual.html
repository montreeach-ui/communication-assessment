Instructions

This questionnaire will help you assess how well you delegate tasks to your team. Taking each pair of statements in turn, choose the number which is most representative of your attitude and behaviour. 1 and 5 are closest to the different statements, 2 and 4 less close but still reflect your behaviour, and 3 is the middle between the two statements.
